# 🧪 Satoshium Simulation Layer

The Satoshium Simulation Layer defines how structured simulation environments
support reasoning, experimentation, coordination modeling, and future
agent-aligned decision workflows within the Satoshium platform.

Simulations provide controlled environments where complex systems can be explored
before they become infrastructure.

---

# 📡 Purpose

The simulation layer enables structured exploration of:

- governance scenarios
- infrastructure coordination pathways
- signal routing environments
- agent interaction models
- knowledge-layer reasoning workflows
- long-horizon system behavior

Its role is to support understanding before deployment.

---

# 🧭 Role Within the Platform

Satoshium is structured as a layered coordination architecture:

satoshium.ai architecture layer
satoshium.net infrastructure layer
satoshium.info knowledge layer
satoshium.xyz experimental layer
satoshium.dev developer layer

Simulation environments typically begin in the experimental layer and migrate
into the developer layer once they become stable reasoning tools.
